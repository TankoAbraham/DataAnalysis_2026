Key findings

Correlation structure: 49 significant correlations were detected among the morphometric traits.


Dimensionality reduction: The first three principal components explain 78.5% of the total variance.


Main contributors: X.5 and X.7 are the strongest contributors to PC1.


Group separation: Some overlap is visible among the groups.

Statistical interpretation
What the PCA shows: PC1, which accounts for 51% of the variance, represents the main axis of morphological variation. Traits with high loadings on this component, especially X.5, X.7, and X.4, are the most influential in distinguishing individuals.
PC2 explains 17.2% of the variance and captures additional variation that is independent of PC1. This axis reflects other morphological differences that are not explained by the primary pattern.
The positions of the group centroids, together with the overlap or separation of the confidence ellipses, indicate whether the groups differ in their morphometric characteristics.
